# Bytewise – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026

Welcome to Bytewise. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Bytewise ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Bytewise, we may collect the following information:

### Account Information
When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:
- Email address.
- Display name and username.
- Profile photograph, if provided.
- Authentication identifiers associated with Google or GitHub sign-in.

### Learning and Application Activity
To provide the learning experience, we may collect:
- Programming courses and lessons accessed.
- Learning progress and completed quizzes.
- Hearts, achievements, and learning statistics.
- App preferences and subscription status.

### User-Generated Content
If you use community features, we may process:
- Posts and comments.
- Usernames and profile information displayed with contributions.
- Other content you voluntarily submit.

### Device and Advertising Information
Our advertising and application services may process:
- Device identifiers and advertising IDs.
- Device model and operating system.
- IP address and approximate location derived from IP.
- Advertising interactions and app usage information.
- Diagnostic and crash information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Authenticate users through Google and GitHub.
- Save learning progress and synchronize information.
- Provide courses, quizzes, and coding exercises.
- Enable community posts and comments.
- Provide Bytewise Pro subscription features.
- Display advertisements and support app monetization.
- Improve application performance and reliability.
- Detect fraud, abuse, and security issues.
- Respond to support requests.

Bytewise uses third-party services to provide its functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

### Firebase (Google)
We use Firebase services for authentication, database functionality, storage, and application infrastructure.

Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Users may authenticate using their Google accounts.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Bytewise may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, and advertising interaction information to provide and measure advertisements.

Bytewise may offer optional paid subscriptions, including Bytewise Pro.

Bytewise provides programming exercises and a coding playground. Code submitted for execution may be transmitted to third-party code execution infrastructure to process the request and return the result.

Users should not include passwords, private keys, personal information, or other confidential information in submitted code.
